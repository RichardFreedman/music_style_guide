

### Figures, Diagrams, and Images

If you are discussing some image (a painting of a performance, for instance), or figure (a chart of key relationships), or other visual information, this too should be documented in your work.

#### Authoritative Sources

For images, you should seek out authorized images from museums or collections, such as those assembled on:

>[ArtStor](https://www.artstor.org/), which is easily available via Tripod<br>
>[The Metropolitan Museum](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access)<br>
>[Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/activating-smithsonian-open-access/)<br>
>[The Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/open-access)<br>
>[The Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access)<br>
>[The National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/open-access-images.html)<br>
>[The Smithsonian Institution](https://www.si.edu/openaccess)<br>


> **Do not use unattributed or unathorized images** downloaded from the internet!  See Fair Use principles in the [Examples and Fair Use](5_examples_intro.md) section of this Style Guide.


#### Make Your Own Diagram

Diagrams, charts, or other custom markup can be created with programs like Google Draw. 

Save your figure in PNG format for use in Word, or Gdocs, etc.

Unless figures are prohibitively large, label them as follows and include them in the body of your essay. Note the use of boldface, and be sure to number figures separately from examples.

#### The Body Reference

Refer to the figure in the body of your prose, explaining what you want the reader to learn from it.  For instance:

>"In this famous image from the sixteenth century, note how the lutenist seems to affect the conditions of melancholia:  distracted, even possessed by his own music making, while the other figures look on, unmoved (see Figure 1)

#### The List of Figures

A list of figures goes at the end of the paper. For example

> List of Figures
> Figure 1. Cariani, Italian. c. 1518-1520. *A Concert*. Painting. The National Gallery of Art (Washington, D.C.), http://library.artstor.org/asset/ANGAIG_10313966926
